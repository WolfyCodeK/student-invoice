## Display changes (1 to 7)

1. **Drums.** The instrument list says "Drums", and so do the register and the
   family editor. The value stored and used in emails stays "drum", so emails
   still read "Finn's drum lessons" and "Invoice for Drum Lessons".
2. **"Week beginning".** *Dropped on 2026-10-01: the main user is happy with
   "Day", so the owner asked for the setting to be removed. It never shipped.*
   A setting renames the register's Day column header
   to "Week beginning". It is off by default, because the column shows each
   family's lesson day.
3. **Paid and Thank you sent.** There are two tick columns to the left of Day,
   one tick per family per half-term. Ticks can be changed at any time,
   including on earlier half-terms.
4. **Half-term total.** A total row sits at the foot of the register. It shows
   the lessons, what's paid, what's outstanding, the total, and "N of M paid".
   The blue band shows the total and the outstanding amount.
5. **Earlier half-terms.** The half-term buttons in the blue band become
   clickable, with arrows to change school year. Half-terms that haven't
   started can't be opened.
   - While a half-term is current, its register is saved as it changes: each
     family's name, pupils, instrument, lesson day, cost, the lessons charged
     and their total.
   - When the half-term ends, that saved state is kept as the final record, so
     a later change of price, family or setting does not alter what was
     charged. This follows the owner's rule: "the final state of the selected
     lessons, and assume drafts will always be sent each term".
   - Deleting a family keeps its earlier records.
   - Half-terms before v1.1.2 were never saved. They are worked out from
     today's families and prices, and an amber strip says so.
   - The lessons of an earlier half-term can't be changed. Only its Paid and
     Thank you sent ticks can.

   **A permanent record (the owner, 2026-09-30).** The main user must be able
   to come back in five years and see every lesson that was invoiced. So:
   - **Each half-term's record holds everything.** That means the half-term's
     dates and every family's details, lesson day and cost. It also holds every
     lesson date, charged or not (with the reason when a lesson wasn't charged:
     unticked or bank holiday), the total, and Paid and Thank you sent. It also
     keeps which charging options were on.
   - **Records are never deleted.** No button removes them. Deleting a family,
     changing a price or setting, editing term dates or clearing the email
     wording never touches a saved record.
   - **Records are always backed up.** They live in the app's data, so they
     are in every automatic backup (daily, before an update, before an import
     or restore, before a delete) and in every Export.
   - **Import and "Restore from backup" merge them.** A file or backup
     replaces the families and settings as today. For records, though, every
     half-term in either copy is kept, and where both have the same half-term,
     the one saved most recently wins. Restoring an older backup can never lose
     newer history.
   - **Size is not a concern.** Five years of weekly lessons for twenty
     families is a few hundred kilobytes.
6. **Term dates.** Settings shows one term-dates section, for the current
   school year only, worked out from today's date. It switches to the new
   school year on 1 August, so the dates are filled in over the summer. The
   "Reset to the usual dates" button and its confirmation go, because
   half-term dates change every year and there are no usual ones. "Undo
   changes" stays. The introduction says the dates start as the app's best
   guess and should be checked against the school's calendar. Dates already
   saved for other years keep working.
7. **Taskbar icon.** The artwork is re-centred on its tile. Today its centre
   is at 47% across in the full-size icon and 42% at 32 px. The icons are
   then regenerated.

## Billing options (8 to 10), all off by default

The three options sit in a new Settings group, "How lessons are charged".
Each option says in plain words what it changes.

### 8. Only charge lessons inside the half-term (parked decision 1)

**Rule.** A lesson is charged for every date from the first day to the last
day of the half-term, inclusive, that falls on the family's lesson day. The
total is still count × cost per lesson, and the date range still runs from the
first to the last charged lesson. Unticking still works on top.

**Today**, every lesson day is charged the number of weeks the half-term
spans, rounded up. With the app's dates this charges some families for a
lesson after the half-term has ended: Monday families in every half-term of
2026/27. The full before-and-after tables are Table A of the
[billing rules proposal](2026-09-billing-v1.1.md). For example:

| 2026/27, Monday, £25 | Today | With the option |
|---|---|---|
| 1st half autumn (1 Sep – 25 Oct) | 8 lessons, £200.00, Mon 7 Sep – Mon 26 Oct | 7 lessons, £175.00, Mon 7 Sep – Mon 19 Oct |
| 2nd half autumn (3 Nov – 20 Dec) | 7 lessons, £175.00, to Mon 21 Dec | 6 lessons, £150.00, to Mon 14 Dec |

With the app's dates, only Monday families change in 2026/27. In 2027/28
Tuesday families change from September to Easter, and Wednesday families in
the summer, because 29 February 2028 moves the weekdays on. (Corrected during
implementation; this sentence first said "only Tuesday families in 2027/28".
The rule is unchanged, and the test pins every half-term.) The register already shows the extra lesson in
its hatched "after the half-term ends" column; with the option on, that
lesson isn't charged.

### 9. Don't charge lessons on bank holidays (parked decision 3)

**Rule.** A lesson that falls on an England and Wales bank holiday is not
charged, exactly as if it had been unticked. The register shows it unticked,
labelled "Bank holiday". If the lesson went ahead anyway, clicking it charges
it again, and that choice is remembered for that family.

**Bank holidays** are worked out by the app, with no internet needed:
- New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, and the first and last Mondays
  of May;
- the last Monday of August, Christmas Day and Boxing Day;
- the substitute days when these fall at a weekend;
- the one-off holidays announced so far (2011, 2012, 2020, 2022, 2023).

A new one-off holiday needs an app update.

**Where it makes a difference**, with the app's usual term dates:

| Half-term | Lesson day | Bank holiday | Today → with the option |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd half spring 2027 | Friday | Good Friday, 26 Mar 2027 | 5 → 4 lessons |
| 2nd half spring 2027 | Monday | Easter Monday, 29 Mar 2027 (after the half-term; only charged today because of item 8) | 5 → 4 lessons |
| 1st half summer 2027 | Monday | Early May, 3 May 2027 | 6 → 5 lessons (5 → 4 with item 8) |
| 1st half summer 2028 | Monday | Easter Monday 17 Apr and Early May 1 May 2028 | 6 → 4 lessons |
| 1st half summer 2028 | Friday | Good Friday, 14 Apr 2028 | 6 → 5 lessons |

With the school's real dates entered, Easter usually falls in the holiday,
so in practice Early May is the one that matters.

**Date line.** It keeps reading as it does now, from the first charged
lesson "to and including" the last. A bank holiday in the middle doesn't
change it. One at either end moves the end in, as unticking does today.

### 10. In the holidays, show the next half-term (parked decision 4)

**Rule.** Outside term time, the register shows the next half-term to start,
headed "starts Tuesday 3 November" for example, and its invoices can be
copied and drafted as usual. **Today** the register says "It's outside term
time" and no invoice can be made until the half-term begins.

### 11. The date line (parked question 4)

No change: it reads "Monday 7th September to and including Monday 26th
October" as now.

## Data (additive only)

Everything new goes inside `settings`, or inside each family's template. It
travels with Export and Import, is included in the automatic backups, and
older versions ignore it (see [data model](../data-model.md) and
[compatibility](../compatibility.md)).

- `settings.dayColumnLabel`: `"day"` (default) or `"weekBeginning"`. (Dropped
  with item 2; never shipped.)
- `settings.charging`: `{ insideHalfTermOnly?, skipBankHolidays?, nextHalfTermInHolidays? }`, all off when absent.
- `settings.halfTerms`: the half-term records, keyed by school year and
  half-term. Each holds, per family: name, pupils, instrument, lesson day,
  cost, lesson dates charged, total, paid, thank-you sent, and a flag once
  the half-term has ended.
- Family template: `chargedBankHolidays?: string[]`, for bank-holiday lessons
  ticked back on.

## What users will notice

- **The main user:** after updating, nothing changes until he switches an
  option on. The new ticks, the half-term total and the earlier half-terms
  appear straight away.
- **A new user:** the same defaults.
